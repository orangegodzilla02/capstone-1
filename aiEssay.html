Ai and job displacement

It is absolutely certain that AI is going to take over some jobs in the future, however how many jobs AI may take over depends on how AI develops in the future. AI is not going to just steal jobs, but also create new ones as well. Jobs that are likely to be replaced by AI usually are jobs that are repeatable, predictable tasks that require little creativity. Jobs that require many different skills, creativity, empathy, and flexibility are less likely to be replaced by AI, though they may be significantly augmented. Most sources as of writing this analysis state that AI will boost the global economy significantly. According to MIT Sloan The global GP is expected to increase by 7 trillion dollars However AI will also significantly disrupt the job market, and impact around 40% of jobs, increasing income inequality.
