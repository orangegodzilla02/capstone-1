Ai and job displacement

It is absolutely certain that AI is going to take over some jobs in the future, however how many jobs AI may take over depends on how AI develops in the future. AI is not going to just steal jobs, but also create new ones as well. Jobs that are likely to be replaced by AI usually are jobs that are repeatable, predictable tasks that require little creativity. jobs that require Many different skills, creativity, and flexibility are less likely to be replaced by AI, though they may be significantly augmented.
